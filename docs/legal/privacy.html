Privacy Policy

Trippzy · Last updated: 8 November 2026

Trippzy ("we", "the app") helps you organise travel bookings. You upload tickets and confirmations; we read them and build your trip. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it, and how you can delete it. The app is operated by Trippzy. Contact: support@trippzy.app.

What we collect

DataWhy
Account details: email address, name, profile picture (if you sign in with Google or Apple), and sign-in credentials handled by our authentication providerTo create and secure your account
Documents you upload: PDFs and photos of tickets, hotel and restaurant bookings, visas and similar travel documents. These can contain personal details such as passenger names, booking references and passport or visa informationTo read them and build your trip, and to let you open them again
Information extracted from your documents: routes, dates, times, providers, booking references, seats, prices, and the text we read from the documentTo show your tickets, itinerary and trip details
Trip details you enter: trip names, destinations, dates, notes, and your editsTo provide the app
Settings: theme and notification preferencesTo remember your choices
Basic technical data: request logs (time, endpoint, error codes) and crash reports, which never include your documents or sign-in tokensSecurity, abuse prevention and fixing bugs

We do not collect your contacts, microphone recordings, or advertising identifiers. The app asks for camera access only if you choose to photograph a ticket. It does not read your photo library; you pick files with the system picker.

Your location (optional)

If you allow it, the app reads your approximate location only while it is open to show a travel note on the Home screen: how far you are from your airport or station and when to leave. Your coordinates are sent to our server for that one request, used to calculate the route (via our routing provider) and discarded. We do not store them, log them, or keep a location history, and the app never reads your location in the background. You can decline or turn it off in your phone's settings; the rest of the app works without it.

On the Edit-profile screen you can optionally add your gender, age and country, and a profile picture. These are used only to personalise your profile, are stored with your account (the picture is stored by our sign-in provider), and can be cleared at any time. Profile avatars offered in the app are public-domain illustrations by DiceBear.

If you turn on calendar reminders, the app adds events for your tickets to a "Trippzy" calendar on your phone. This is stored only on your phone's calendar.

How your documents are processed

When you upload a document, it is stored privately and a text version is sent to an AI model to extract the booking details. We ask our AI provider not to retain or train on your data. We do not use your documents to train models. The extraction runs on our servers; your phone never holds our AI keys.

Because these parts are in different countries, your data crosses borders when you use the app. All of it is encrypted in transit. These providers act on our behalf. We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Files are stored privately; the app receives short-lived links that expire in minutes. Every request is checked against your account, so other users cannot access your data. If you use the offline feature, copies of tickets for upcoming trips are kept in the app's private storage on your phone and removed when you sign out.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. You can delete a ticket or a trip at any time, which removes the files and details. Deleting your account removes everything described above.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and all data: in the app, Settings → Account → Delete account, or use the account deletion page.
  • Edit or delete any trip or ticket in the app.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data or a correction by emailing the address above.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or India's DPDP Act) you may have further rights to access, correct, port or erase your data and to withdraw consent. Contact us and we will respond.

Children

Trippzy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.
