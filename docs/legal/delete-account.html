Delete your Trippzy account

You can erase your account and everything in it. This cannot be undone.

Option 1 — in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Trippzy and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete, then confirm Delete everything.

Option 2 — by email

If you can no longer open the app, email [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Trippzy account". We will verify it is you and delete your account within 30 days (usually much sooner) and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • All trips, tickets and the details extracted from them
  • Every file you uploaded, including the page images we generated from PDFs
  • Your settings, and the offline copies on your phone when you sign out

What may remain

Security and server logs that do not contain your documents are kept for a short period (up to 30 days) for abuse prevention and then deleted. Backups, if any, are overwritten on their normal cycle.
